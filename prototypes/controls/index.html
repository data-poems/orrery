control studies.

Three ways to reorganize the Solar Orrery HUD. Each demonstrates the new sky-ignite cue — no popup, the control lights and the sky shimmers once.
orbiting bodies01

Controls orbit the sun on live auto-rotating rings, icons staying upright. Hover to pause. The metaphor is the app.
hex cluster02

A flush flat-top honeycomb; tapping a tile blooms a ring of sub-hexes (two-level). Remote-navigable.
radial dial03

A still astrolabe pie wheel: angular wedges around a sun hub with rim ticks. Crisp wedge focus.
selector ring04

A rotary ring you spin with momentum; whichever node snaps into the top slot is the active control.
bottom dock05

The conventional control: a slim always-visible dock along the bottom edge. Frees both corners.
sun hub06

The sun itself is the settings hub: select it and controls bloom radially out, with a second level for sub-options.
corner bloom07

A hub pinned to the bottom-right fans controls up-and-left in a quarter-arc, with a second level for sub-options.
bottom bloom08

A bottom-center hub blooms controls upward in a semicircle, with a second level for sub-options.
