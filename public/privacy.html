Orrery Privacy

Effective November 6, 2026.

Orrery does not collect, store, or sell personal information. There is no account system and no analytics SDK in the app.

Network requests

The app contacts these public services from your device:

  • NASA NeoWs — today's near-Earth object feed, requested each time the app starts, unless today's list is already cached from earlier in the same session
  • JPL Small-Body Database — asteroid orbital elements, requested when you select a near-Earth object
  • NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center — solar wind speed summary, requested each time the app starts
  • CelesTrak — two-line element sets for space stations, requested when you turn on the Satellites layer

Static star, constellation, and deep-sky catalogs ship with the app bundle.

The website also loads its typeface from Google Fonts and records page views with GoatCounter at stats.dr.eamer.dev. The iPhone, iPad, and Android apps do neither.

Local storage

Today's NEO list may be cached in sessionStorage for the current day only. The app may use localStorage for your theme preference, whether the opening cinematic has been handled, and review-prompt eligibility history such as explored destination identifiers, random per-launch identifiers, app versions, and dates. These values remain on your device and are not sent to Luke Steuber.
